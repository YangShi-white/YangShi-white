## Research

My current research explores the use of artificial intelligence for scientific data and knowledge discovery, including:

- **Scientific Data Modeling**  
  Machine learning for bioprocess prediction, process optimization, and data-driven scientific analysis.

- **Scientific Knowledge Graphs**  
  Information extraction from scientific literature, domain knowledge graph construction, relation completion, and knowledge-grounded reasoning.

- **Scientific Information Extraction**  
  Document-level reconstruction of experimental events from fragmented scientific literature with evidence traceability.

- **LLM / Agent for Science**  
  Integration of structured scientific knowledge with large language models for knowledge retrieval, reasoning, and scientific decision support.
